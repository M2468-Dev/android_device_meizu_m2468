Device configuration for Meizu 21 Note
======================================

The Meizu 21 Note (codenamed "m2468") is a mid-range smartphone from Meizu.

It was announced on May 16, 2024 and released on May 20, 2024.

## Device specifications

Basic   | Spec Sheet
-------:|:-------------------------
SoC     | Qualcomm SM8550-AB Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 (4 nm)
CPU     | Octa-core (1x3.19 GHz Cortex-X3 & 2x2.8 GHz Cortex-A715 & 2x2.8 GHz Cortex-A710 & 3x2.0 GHz Cortex-A510)
GPU     | Adreno 740
Memory  | 16 GB LPDDR5X RAM
Shipped Android Version | 14.0 with Flyme OS 10.5.2.0A
Storage | 256/512 GB UFS 3.1
Battery | Non-removable Li-Po 5500 mAh
Display | 6.78-inch Tianma U8 8T LTPO OLED, 1264 x 2780 pixels, 19.8:9 ratio, 1-144Hz, HDR10+, 800 nits (typ), 1600 nits (HBM), 5000 nits (peak)
Rear Camera | 50 MP (Wide, OIS) + 13 MP (Ultra-wide)
Front Camera | 16 MP
Fingerprint | Under-display optical fingerprint sensor
Charging | 66W wired
WLAN | Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac/ax/be, Wi-Fi 7, dual-band
Bluetooth | Bluetooth 5.3
NFC | Yes
IR Blaster | Yes
